README_MODIFICATIONS

11/12/25
Artemis has been modified from its original form in the following manner:
com\google\common and META-INF\maven\com.google.guava folders were deleted.
org\apache\xalan (2.7.2) was replaced with an updated version (2.7.3).
org\apache\common\compress and META-INF\maven\org.apache.commons\commons-compress folders were deleted.
The following sub-folders in the org\apache\batik folder were deleted: anim, bridge, css, dom, gvt, i18n, parser, script, transcoder, xml. The META-INF\maven\org.apache.xmlgraphics folder was also deleted.
